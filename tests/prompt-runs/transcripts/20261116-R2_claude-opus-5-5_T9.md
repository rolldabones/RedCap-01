## 6. Recommendations

All items below are future-state proposals, not observations.

**R1. Name an owner for the objective.**
Accountability for winning the two contracts gives risk owners a single person to escalate to. It also makes trade-offs across risks someone's job. The choice of person is a management decision; the CEO or a designated aerospace-growth executive would be natural candidates.

**R2. Route risk-owner input into the bid decision.**
Require the owners of objective-relevant risks to confirm, before CEO approval, that their risks have been reflected in the bid. Certification delay (Quality Director) and supplier concentration (Head of Procurement) are the obvious first candidates. This puts risk insight into the decision while options are still open, rather than recording it alongside. Add it to the existing bid-approval step; no new document is needed.

**R3. Assign an owner to each critical bid assumption, starting with certification timelines.**
An owned assumption can be challenged and updated. An unowned one tends to persist until it fails. Proposal: the Quality Director owns certification-timeline assumptions, because that person already owns the related risk.

**R4. Replace "monitor" with a trigger, an action and an action owner for objective-relevant risks.**
This converts passive observation into a pre-agreed response, so reassessment happens on evidence rather than on calendar or memory. Extend the existing register's columns rather than creating a new artifact.
