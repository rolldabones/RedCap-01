## 8. Longer-Term Improvements

All items are recommendations.

1. **Embed risk in the annual planning cycle and budget process.** Plans and budgets for each objective should state critical assumptions, boundaries and triggers. Resources then follow risk-informed priorities.
2. **Map the register to objectives.** Retire or merge entries that do not affect any objective. This focuses effort on what changes outcomes.
3. **Use scenarios or ranges for the ED objective's key uncertainties.** Candidate areas are demand, staffing and patient flow; these are illustrative and require validation. Highlight interdependencies such as staffing and triage throughput. This avoids the false precision of single scores.
4. **Define a clear ownership model.** Assign named owners for decisions, assumptions, triggers and actions alongside objective owners. Accountability then sits where action is taken.
5. **Establish a learning loop.** Review major decisions at an interval to be set, asking whether assumptions held, whether triggers fired in time and what changed. Feed the results into planning.
6. **Track ERM value explicitly.** Record decisions changed, earlier pivots and losses avoided, and report them to the board. This demonstrates whether ERM is improving decisions.
