## 6. Recommendations

All items below are future-state recommendations, not current practice.

**R1. Put the fee-model decision through a pre-decision risk review now, before approval.**
- Frame two or three scenarios for client response, for example broad acceptance, partial pushback and significant attrition. Express retention impact as ranges.
- Set conditions for proceeding, and a fallback, for each scenario.
- *Why it improves decisions:* it brings ERM in while options are still open, and tests the critical assumption before commitment.

**R2. Convert "moderate appetite for change" into decision boundaries for the retention objective.** See the register below.
- *Why:* decision-makers get an unambiguous point at which to continue, adjust or stop. Escalation stops depending on judgment in the moment.

**R3. Define and embed triggers for the fee-model assumption and for retention.** See the register below.
- Run them through existing commercial reporting, not a separate risk process.
- *Why:* failure is detected early enough to pivot, rather than discovered when the annual retention rate is reported.

**R4. Assess the combined client impact of the CRM transition and the fee change.**
- Confirm the timing of both.
- If they overlap, decide deliberately whether to sequence them, buffer them or proceed together.
- *Why:* it addresses a possible compounding risk to the single objective that both decisions serve.

**R5. Name decision owners and action owners, and confirm independent challenge of the fee-model assumption.**
- If the CCO sponsors the fee change, assign an independent reviewer of the assumption. The CRO or Internal Audit are options for management to decide.
- *Why:* accountability is clear, and confirmation bias on the most consequential assumption is reduced.

**R6. Move the ERM review upstream for material decisions.**
- Require risk input at option framing, before the business case is finalized.
- Do this by adding a decision gate, not a new document.
- *Why:* ERM can influence which options are considered, not only score the one already chosen.

**R7. Re-anchor the quarterly report on the objective.**
- Show the retention trajectory against boundaries, the status of the critical assumption, triggers fired and actions taken. Show heat-map movement only where it is relevant.
- *Why:* executive and board discussion shifts from risk listings to whether the objective is on track and what must be decided.

**R8. Run a post-decision learning review of the CRM replacement.**
- Ask what ERM knew, when, and whether it could have changed the decision.
- Set conditions to monitor during implementation.
- *Why:* it starts the learning loop and creates the first evidence of ERM value, or the reason value is absent.
