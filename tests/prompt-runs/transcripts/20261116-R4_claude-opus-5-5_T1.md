## 8. Longer-Term Improvements

- **Embed risk review in the decision calendar.** Place it in strategic planning, budgeting and investment approvals, not only in risk reporting.
- **Add opportunity-side risks.** Link them to growth objectives so ERM supports value creation as well as protection.
- **Map interdependencies between risks.** For example, the combined effect of R5 and R2 on stock availability, analyzed through scenarios.
- **Introduce a learning loop.** Review the outcomes of risk-informed decisions periodically and adjust assumptions and thresholds.
- **Report to the board on decisions, not just scores.** Cover which decisions risk information influenced, which triggers fired and what actions followed.
