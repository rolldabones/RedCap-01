## 6. Recommendations

*All items below are future-state recommendations, not observations.*

**R1. Assign an owner to the objective.**
- Management to decide the role.
- *Why it improves decisions:* one person becomes accountable for whether the risk picture across the 25 risks supports winning two contracts, not just for individual risks.

**R2. Bring risk owners into the bid decision.**
- Before CEO approval, the owners of objective-relevant risks (for example certification, supplier and skills) confirm or challenge the bid's assumptions.
- *Why:* risk insight reaches the decision while options are still open, not after commitment.

**R3. Give each critical bid assumption a named owner.**
- Start with certification timelines. A logical candidate is the Quality Director, given ownership of certification delay (assumption; management to confirm).
- *Why:* the person best placed to know whether an assumption still holds becomes responsible for saying so.

**R4. Replace "monitor" with owned actions tied to triggers for objective-relevant risks.**
- *Why:* "monitor" names no consequence. A trigger with an owner and a required action converts awareness into timely response.

**R5. Map the 25 risks to the objective.**
- Focus ownership effort on the risks that determine bid success.
- *Why:* this concentrates executive attention without adding documentation for low-relevance risks.

**Recommended Trigger Register (illustrative structure; thresholds to be set by management):**

| Assumption / risk | Trigger (to be defined) | Monitor (recommended) | Required action (recommended) | Decision affected |
