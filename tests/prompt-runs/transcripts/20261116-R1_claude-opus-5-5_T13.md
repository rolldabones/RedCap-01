## 7. Quick Wins

1. **Hold a pre-decision risk session on the fee model** with the CCO before the decision closes. Present scenarios, not a score. *Benefit:* the first chance to change a decision while options are still open.
2. **Agree how the fee-model assumption will be evidenced.** Decide what evidence of client acceptance would confirm or refute it, and by when. *Benefit:* turns a belief into a monitored assumption.
3. **Define one trigger per decision.** *Benefit:* a reason to act exists between quarterly reports.
4. **Reframe the next quarterly report around the retention objective.** Report status against 92%, the state of key assumptions and any trigger status, instead of register content. *Benefit:* executive and board discussion moves from risks to the objective.
5. **Begin recording ERM-influenced decisions.** Record a decision only when ERM input changed it, briefly. *Benefit:* creates the evidence base for demonstrating value. This is decision evidence, not a new report.

## 8. Longer-Term Improvements

1. **Move ERM to the start of business cases** for decisions affecting the retention objective, so risk input shapes options rather than annotates them.
2. **Translate risk appetite into decision boundaries** for each strategic objective, not only retention.

4. **Embed ERM in planning, budgeting, investment and transformation forums,** starting with CRM delivery governance.
5. **Run a post-implementation learning review on the CRM decision.** Compare what the business case assumed with what occurred, and feed the lessons into future decisions.
6. **Shift board reporting from risk inventory to decision oversight.** Report which decisions ERM informed, which triggers fired and what changed as a result.
