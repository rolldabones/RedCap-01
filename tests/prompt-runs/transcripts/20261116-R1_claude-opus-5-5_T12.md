## 6. Recommendations

*Future-state recommendations, not current-state facts.*

**R1. Translate appetite into decision boundaries for the expansion objective.**
Define an acceptable range, warning threshold, trigger and required action for each success measure. This gives the investment committee and the CEO an explicit test for capital allocation and launch decisions, replacing an untestable qualitative statement.

**R2. Define triggers for launch timing and critical assumptions, with owners and pre-agreed responses.**
Pre-committed responses shorten decision time and remove debate at the moment of stress. Acceleration triggers allow the organisation to capture the upside scenario.

**R3. Require ERM input before investment recommendations are finalised.**
For future expansion and major capital decisions, the investment committee should receive scenario-based risk input while options are still open. This addresses the market-entry pattern directly.

**R4. Reframe scenario reviews around actions.**
For each scenario, agree the management actions, the decision owner and the point at which they apply. This shifts executive discussion from scoring to choosing responses.

**R5. Assign named owners to the four critical decisions, three assumptions and all triggers.**
Clear ownership ensures that a signal leads to action rather than to a report.

**R6. Extend the method to value creation.**
Use the upside scenario and partner and vendor strengths to identify opportunities ERM can enable. This demonstrates that ERM supports growth as well as protection.

## 7. Quick Wins

1. **Set appetite thresholds for the two moderate-confidence assumptions and launch timing.** These carry the highest exposure. The six- and twelve-month scenario markers already provide the anchors.
2. **Name owners** for the four critical decisions and three assumptions. This costs almost nothing and creates immediate accountability.
3. **Add a standing ERM item to the next investment committee and monthly performance review**, focused on trigger status rather than risk scores.
4. **Re-run the regulatory scenario discussion as an action session.** Agree what management does under the expected, downside and upside cases.
5. **Confirm and record the remediation status** of the cybersecurity integration weaknesses before implementation proceeds.

## 8. Longer-Term Improvements

1. **Enterprise-wide decision boundaries.** Translate the risk appetite framework into measurable boundaries for all strategic objectives, not only this expansion.
2. **Decision gating.** Make ERM input a formal precondition for investment recommendations, so risk shapes options rather than reviewing them afterwards.
3. **Assumption drift monitoring.** Build a routine process with owners, indicators and review in the normal operating rhythm.
4. **Integration into transformation governance and budgeting.** This moves ERM from a reporting cycle to the decision cycle.
5. **Value evidence.** Track decisions changed, pivots made and opportunities enabled, and report them to the board so ERM's contribution is visible.
6. **Post-decision learning.** Review the market-entry decision against actual outcomes to complete the governance loop.
