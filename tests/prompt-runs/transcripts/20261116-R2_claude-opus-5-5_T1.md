1. **Add an "objective affected" column and an "owner" column to the existing register.** This is low effort and immediately makes the register usable in decisions.
2. **Run a single test:** identify the last major decision Ashgrove made and ask whether the register was consulted and whether it changed anything. The answer is direct evidence for Decision linkage and Value.
3. **For R1 and R4, define one trigger and one required action each.** These are the two highest calculated scores. Doing so tests whether the trigger approach works before scaling it.
4. **Document the scoring scale definitions** so that likelihood and impact scores mean the same thing to every user.

## 8. Longer-Term Improvements

1. **Embed ERM in strategy, planning, budgeting and investment cycles**, so risk input arrives before options close rather than in after-the-fact reporting.
2. **Introduce scenario analysis** for interdependent risks, particularly the possible fulfilment cluster (R1, R2, R5).
3. **Add opportunity and upside analysis** alongside threats.
4. **Build assumption monitoring.** Identify the assumptions behind key plans, such as supplier stability or the regulatory trajectory, and give each an owner.
5. **Establish a learning loop.** After incidents, near-misses and major decisions, record what ERM got right or wrong and adjust.
6. **Shape board reporting around objectives and decisions** rather than heat-map scores.
