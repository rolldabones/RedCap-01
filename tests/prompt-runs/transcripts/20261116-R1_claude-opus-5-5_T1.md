## 6. Recommendations

*Future-state recommendations. Nothing here describes Ashgrove's current practice.*

**R-A. Start from objectives, not the register.**
- Have the executive team name its 3–5 most important objectives for the planning period. Each objective needs an owner and a measure.
- Then map each register entry to the objective(s) it affects. Entries that map to no objective should be challenged.
- *Why it improves decisions:* risk is then discussed in terms executives already decide on, such as revenue, margin, customer and compliance outcomes.

**R-B. Identify the decisions that matter.**
- For each objective, list the 2–3 upcoming decisions that most determine success, for example platform investment, supplier concentration or credit product strategy (illustrative, not facts).
- Bring risk input into those decisions before options close.
- *Why:* this moves ERM from reporting to influence.

**R-C. Assign explicit owners.**

- *Why:* ownership determines whether anything happens when conditions change.
