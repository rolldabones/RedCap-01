**R1. Translate each appetite category into boundaries for the specific decisions it governs, rather than enterprise-wide abstractions.**
*Why it improves decisions:* Each decision-maker gets a test they can apply before choosing, not after.

*Why it improves decisions:* It removes ad hoc trade-offs at the point of decision, and it makes the board's protection priorities enforceable.

*Why it improves decisions:* A trigger without an owner does not produce action.

**R5. Embed boundary checks in existing forums (the monthly pricing cycle and billing migration governance) rather than creating a separate risk report.**
