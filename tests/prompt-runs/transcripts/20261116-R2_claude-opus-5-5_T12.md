## 6. Recommendations

**R1. Translate "moderate appetite for expansion" into decision boundaries tied to the objective's success measures** (see register below).

**R2. Require ERM input before investment recommendations are finalized.**
The partner-selection pattern (risk input during selection) becomes the standard for investment committee submissions.
*Why it improves decisions:* ERM shapes options rather than reviewing conclusions. This closes G1.

**R3. Define owned triggers with required responses for regulatory timing, partner performance and supply capacity** (see register below).
*Why it improves decisions:* It shortens the time from signal to pivot and makes acceleration a planned option rather than an improvisation.
