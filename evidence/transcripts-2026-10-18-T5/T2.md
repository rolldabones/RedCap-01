## 1. Executive Summary

Marrowfield's ERM starts from an explicit, measurable and owned objective. It has produced a documented, decision-changing outcome: the dual-running period added after the March scenario analysis limited lost deliveries in July 2026. Few ERM programs can show a traceable chain from analysis to decision to avoided loss.

Four issues limit confidence that ERM will consistently protect the objective:

1. **The packaging supplier decision bypassed risk review.** This decision sits on the one interdependency the scenario pack explicitly flags: a single supplier feeding a single plant.
2. **Half of the objective has no decision boundary.** OTIF, the delivery constraint in the objective, has no acceptable range, warning, trigger or required action. Complaints per million units have no target.
3. **Trigger escalation timing conflicts with the required action.** Breaches route to a *monthly* review, but the utilization action requires the COO to act within *five business days*.
4. **The downside scenario misses the objective, and no pre-agreed response exists.** Downside margin is 8.5% against a 10% target, and downside utilization of 97% exceeds the 93% trigger.

The 2027 capital budget (November 2026) is the next point where options close. It is the most valuable place to address these issues.
