| No decision linkage | Risks that inform no decision cannot improve decisions. |

## 7. Recommendations (Future State)

1. **Anchor every risk to a named objective.** This turns "what could go wrong" into "what could stop us achieving X." Executives then weigh risks against outcomes they care about.
2. **Identify the three to five decisions that most determine success this year,** and map risks to them. This lets risk analysis reach a decision while options are still open.
3. **Replace or supplement scores with triggers and decision boundaries** for the top risks. This shifts attention from rating risks to knowing when to act.
4. **Assign owners to objectives, assumptions, triggers and actions,** not just to risks. Accountability then sits with the people who make the decisions.
5. **Add opportunities.** Examples could include upside from e-commerce growth or from regulatory change in credit. This makes ERM relevant to strategy, not only to compliance.
