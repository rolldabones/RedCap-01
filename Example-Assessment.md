# Findings

## Strengths

- A clear, owned strategic objective with a measurable revenue target.
- Vendor due diligence that changed a decision before it closed.
- Executive sponsorship and regular board reporting.

---

## Gaps

- Risk reached the market-entry decision after the investment recommendation.
- Risk appetite has no measurable definition, so it cannot bound a decision.
- Triggers are informal and none carries a response.
- Assumptions have no stated owners and are not monitored.
- Owners of decisions, assumptions, boundaries, triggers and actions: Unknown.

---

## Recommendations

Prioritize practical improvements. Each says why it improves a decision.

### Immediate

- Name an owner for each of the three assumptions, so that a drift in approval timing or partner performance reaches someone who can act on the launch and capital decisions.
- Translate the expansion appetite into boundaries for the capital and launch decisions. Recommended, for management to set: capital overrun above 10% goes to executive review; regulatory delay beyond 90 days reopens the launch schedule; partner service levels below the agreed minimum activate a contingency partner. The values are recommendations, not facts of the case.

### Near Term

- Put risk on the investment committee agenda before recommendations are made, so that risk can change the option chosen rather than describe it afterwards.
- Replace likelihood scoring of the scenarios with the action management would take under each, so that the scenarios inform the launch-timing decision.

### Longer Term

- Record which decisions ERM changed, and review them after the fact, so that the program is judged by decisions improved rather than artifacts produced.

# Management Questions

1. Who owns the market-entry, capital and launch-timing decisions, and with what decision rights?
2. What acceptable range, warning threshold and required action does "moderate appetite for expansion" mean for capital and for timing?
3. Who will monitor the regulatory-approval and partner-performance assumptions, and how often?
